Developer Work Rhythm Study (Academic Research — 90 seconds)

── FORM DESCRIPTION ────────────────────────────────────────────
I'm a student researcher studying how GitHub commit patterns relate to developer work rhythms. This 5-question survey takes about 90 seconds. Your responses are used only for academic research and will never be shared publicly. A GitHub username is optional but helps match your responses to commit data.

Reference: Adan & Almirall (1991). Personality and Individual Differences.
